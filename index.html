Welcome to the official Btekhnay Municipality site

Btekhnay is a picturesque village nestled in the mountains of Lebanon, showcasing our vibrant events, stunning landscapes, and the rich culture of our community.

Join us as we celebrate our heritage and share the beauty of Btekhnay through various activities and events that highlight our unique municipality.
