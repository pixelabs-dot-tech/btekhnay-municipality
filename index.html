Btekhnay is a picturesque village nestled in the mountains of Lebanon, showcasing our vibrant events, stunning landscapes, and the rich culture of our community.

Discover our village اكتشف قريتنا

Join us as we celebrate our heritage and share the beauty of Btekhnay through various activities and events that highlight our unique municipality.

Experience local beauty جمال الطبيعة

In the 1960s and early '70s, families from Beirut and across the Arab world spent their summers here for the cool mountain air and the views. The village is rich in underground water and mountain springs.

  • Clock Tower
  • Martyrs' Square
  • Town Hall
  • Mountain springs
